# Gauntlet — Christ the Redeemer in Three.js

> Faithful English translation of [GAUNTLET.md](GAUNTLET.md). The Portuguese
> original is the historical record; filenames, dates, raw results, and measured
> values are intentionally preserved here without reinterpretation.

At most three rounds: build, capture, compare with `ref/`, score each item, and
record what could not be resolved. No number in this document was estimated.
Values come from `shots/<round>/metrics.json` and `shots/<round>/test-ui.json`,
generated by `scripts/capture.mjs` and `scripts/test-ui.mjs`.

## Measurement conditions

**Machine:** iMac Pro 2017 — Xeon W-2140B 8c/16t at 3.2 GHz, 32 GB, Radeon Pro
Vega 56 8 GB, macOS 15.6 (Darwin 24.6).

**Measured renderer:** `ANGLE (AMD, ANGLE Metal Renderer: AMD Radeon Pro Vega
56)`. This is the real GPU, not SwiftShader; every round records it in
`metrics.json`.

**Browser:** Chromium 1228 (Playwright 1.61 `chromium` channel), new headless
mode, `--use-angle=metal --enable-gpu --disable-frame-rate-limit`, 1920×1080,
DPR 1. Capture was sequential: one browser per angle, opened and closed before
the next, so headless instances did not contend for the GPU.

| condition | method | calibration |
|---|---|---|
| native | no throttling | fixed 4M-iteration loop: **10.5 ms** |
| throttled | `Emulation.setCPUThrottlingRate: 4` | same loop: **43.7 ms**, 4.16× slower |

The calibration explains nearly equal frame rates: the scene was GPU-bound, not
CPU-bound. Network testing emulated fast 4G through CDP (4 Mbit/s down, 3 up,
20 ms latency) with a cold cache (`cache-control: no-store`).

## Round 2 — 2026-08-18, 12:53

**Machine state:** `up 1:59`, load 1.79 / 2.05 / 2.28. `mediaanalysisd` used
about 30% of one core during the round. This is recorded because it differs from
round 1; the scene remained GPU-bound, but frame rate fell from 155 to 144.
See `shots/r2/estado-maquina.txt`.

### Changes from round 1

1. The cloud bank gained noise-gradient shading, billow noise, and valley fill
   (`src/cloudfill.js`) using the same noise as the sheet. This hides the
   sheet-to-mesh intersection.
2. Hemispheric light rose from 0.78 to 1.45 at 17:30, so shadowed stone reads
   as stone rather than silhouette.
3. Night spotlights were reduced from 1400 to 320, widened, softened, and aimed
   2 m higher.
4. Cloud coverage was recalibrated by time of day: 0.12 at noon, 0.48 at dawn,
   and 0.38 at 17:30.
5. The GLB fetch moved to the document head, the import map moved before module
   preloads, and heavy world construction waits for the statue.
6. Mobile layout was rebuilt to avoid HUD, bar, and credit overlap.
7. `C-base` and `D-ambiente` were reframed; `F-escala` was added.

### Measurement correction

Round 1 used `--disable-frame-rate-limit` during load measurement. Controlled
A/B testing found that this delayed first frame with the statue by about 3.6 s;
the GPU queue saturated and delayed shader compilation. The flag remains only
for frame-cost testing. Round-1 and round-2 load values are therefore not
directly comparable; round 2 is the valid load result.

## Addendum — cycle mode (2026-08-18, after the gauntlet)

Victor added a mode that keeps rotation active and advances time one hour per
second, completing a day in 24 seconds. The authoring prompt gained item 18;
the original 34-point rounds were not rescored.

### Item 18 — score 2

| verification | measured result |
|---|---|
| time advances about 1 h/s | **3.02 h in 3.0 s** (5.30 → 8.32) |
| rotation remains active | azimuth changed **−0.122 rad**, `ciclo=true` |
| toggle stops cycle and freezes time | 8.35 → **8.35** in 1.2 s, `ciclo=false` |
| `ciclo=1` link starts rotating immediately | `idle` begins above the 4 s threshold |

`shots/r2/test-ui.json` records **17/17** checks, with the final three covering
cycle mode.

| condition | mean fps | median frame | p95 | draw calls |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| rotation only (round 2) | 144.3 | 3.10 ms | 14.50 ms | 11 |
| **rotation + cycle** | **126.7** | 3.10 ms | **16.30 ms** | 11–14 |

The median is unchanged because the cost is shadow-map updates, which occur
when the sun moves 0.15 hours (about seven times per second), not every frame.
During the cycle, URL hash writes are reduced from the normal 350 ms debounce to
every two seconds to remain below Safari's `replaceState` rate limit. The HUD's
11–14 draw-call range includes the frames that execute the shadow pass.

`scripts/serve.mjs` was also changed to choose the next free port rather than
fail with `EADDRINUSE`, allowing measurement while a local scene server exists.
